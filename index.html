AA-QAWF in Motion

Two animated figures for the board deck: one EPIC travelling through the eight phases and five human checkpoints, and the three-layer architecture: knowledge bank, orchestrator, execution. Embed this page in Gamma or record a stage with Present.

Using these in Gamma

  1. Embed: add a web embed block in Gamma and paste this page's link. Add ?figure=pipeline or ?figure=arch to show one figure filling the frame, and &theme=dark or &theme=light to match the deck.
  2. Record: press Present on a figure, wait for the controls to fade, record the screen, then trim to one loop. Upload the clip as the card's video.
  3. Still: pause at the frame you want and screenshot. Pick Light or Dark to match the deck.
